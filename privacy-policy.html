Binfluencer: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

The short version. Binfluencer doesn't need an account. Your saved addresses and reminder settings stay on your phone. To find your bin days, our server receives the postcode and address you choose, but it never knows who you are. If you share a home with your household, we keep the nickname you choose and, if you allow notifications, a token to send household alerts to your phone. The app currently shows no adverts. If we add small adverts later (from Google AdMob), we'll update this policy first, and you'll choose whether they're personalised.

1. Who we are

Binfluencer is made by Look Behind You Software (LBY Software), run by Stefano Virgili in Scotland. We are responsible for the personal data described in this policy. You can contact us at lookbehindyousoftware@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your phone

The app keeps the following on your device only:

  • the addresses you save and the names you give them (for example "Home");
  • your reminder settings;
  • the latest collection dates for your addresses, so the app works without a signal;
  • which councils you've voted for, and any you've asked to hear about when they arrive;
  • your advertising consent choice, once adverts are added (see section 8).

This is removed when you delete an address in the app or uninstall the app.

3. Looking up your bin days

  • Your postcode. When you type a postcode, the app sends it to our server. Our server uses postcodes.io, an open postcode service, to work out which council covers it, then gets the list of addresses for that postcode from the council's website. We keep that list of addresses for up to 7 days so we don't have to ask the council again every time. Some councils, such as the City of Edinburgh Council, publish street lists rather than address lists. For those, postcodes.io also gives us the location of your postcode, and we match it to streets using our own copy of the council's street list and open map data from OpenStreetMap. We already hold that data, so nothing about you is sent to OpenStreetMap or to the council.
  • Your address. When you pick your address, our server stores the address, postcode, the council's reference number for the property and its collection dates, so it can keep the dates up to date. This record is not linked to your name, email, phone number or device, and everyone who picks the same address shares the same record.
  • Councils we don't cover yet. If your council isn't supported yet, the app sends your postcode so our server can work out which council it is, then adds one vote for that council. We only store the council and its number of votes. We don't keep your postcode, and each phone votes once per council.
  • Hearing when your council arrives. If you ask us to tell you when your council is added, that choice stays on your phone. The app checks our public list of supported councils and shows a notification on your phone when yours appears. We don't receive your details, a device ID or a notification token.

Want your council included? Email us at lookbehindyousoftware@gmail.com with the name of your council, and we'll see what we can do. We'll only use your email to reply to you.

4. Keeping your dates up to date

The app checks our server for changes when you open it and in the background about twice a day. Our server checks the council's website on a schedule. Your phone never contacts council websites itself, unless you tap a link to your council's guidance, which opens it in your browser.

5. Reminders and sharing a home

Night-before reminders are scheduled by your phone itself. If you don't share a home, we don't receive a notification token, a device ID or anything else to send them, and the "last call" nudge is scheduled by your phone too. You can turn reminders and nudges off in the app or in your phone's settings at any time.

If you share a home with the people you live with, or join one with a code, our server keeps:

  • the nickname you type (for example "Jamie"), so the others know who took the bins out;
  • which home the household is for, and its share code (codes stop working after 7 days);
  • who tapped "Done, bins are out" for each collection day, and when;
  • your last call nudge setting (on or off, and the time);
  • if you allow notifications, your phone's push notification token, and whether it's an iPhone or Android phone;
  • a scrambled (hashed) copy of the secret code your phone uses to prove it's part of the household.

We use this only to run the household: to tell the others when someone taps Done or joins, and to send one last call nudge if nobody has. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number. When you leave a household, or someone who shared it removes you, your membership and push token are deleted; when the last person leaves, the household is deleted too. Records of which nudges were sent are deleted after 30 days.

6. Calendar subscription

If you choose to add your collections to your calendar, the app gives your calendar a web link for that address. Your calendar app (for example Apple Calendar or Google Calendar) will then fetch the dates from our server regularly. Anyone who has the link can see the collection dates for that address, so only share it with people you choose.

7. Technical logs and fair use

Like most online services, our hosting provider keeps short-lived technical logs, which include IP addresses, to keep the service secure and working. We don't use these to identify you, and they are kept for a short period only.

To stop anyone overloading the service or guessing household codes, our server limits how many requests one connection can make in a short time. To count them it keeps a scrambled (hashed) version of the IP address, never the address itself, next to a number. These counters are deleted after about a day, and we can't turn them back into an IP address.

8. Advertising

At the moment Binfluencer shows no adverts, and Google AdMob is not part of the app. We plan to add small adverts in a later update, with an option to pay once to remove them. Before that happens we'll update this page and the date at the top. This section describes how it will work.

Binfluencer will stay free, with small adverts provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure adverts, AdMob will collect your device's advertising ID, your IP address, device details and how you interact with the adverts.

  • In the UK and EEA, the app asks for your consent before any personalised adverts are shown. If you say no, you'll only see non-personalised adverts, which still use some data to work and to prevent fraud.
  • On iPhone, Apple will ask whether you allow the app to track you. If you choose "Ask App not to Track", your advertising ID isn't shared.
  • You can change your choice at any time in the app, and reset or limit your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Google's use of this data is covered by Google's privacy policy. We never share your addresses or postcodes with advertisers, we don't use any separate analytics or tracking tools, and we never sell your data.

9. Third parties

  • Supabase hosts our server and database, on servers in London, UK.
  • Google AdMob will provide the adverts once we add them (see section 8).
  • postcodes.io receives the postcode from our server to find your council.
  • Expo passes household notifications from our server to Apple or Google, which deliver them to your phone. Only used if you share a home and allow notifications.
  • Your council's website is where collection dates come from. Our server requests them, so the council does not receive any information about you.
  • Apple and Google provide the app stores and the notification system on your phone, and your calendar if you subscribe to one. Their own privacy policies apply.

10. Legal basis

Under UK data protection law, we process postcodes and addresses because we have a legitimate interest in providing the service you asked for: showing your bin collection dates. The same applies to household nicknames, push tokens and Done taps if you choose to share a home. We use them for nothing else. Personalised adverts are based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

11. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete personal data, withdraw your advertising consent, or object to how we use it. Because we don't know who you are, the quickest way to remove your data is to delete the address in the app (which also takes you out of its household, if you share it) or to leave the household in Settings. To have an address record removed from our server, email us with the address. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

12. Children

Binfluencer is not aimed at children, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change how Binfluencer uses data, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
