Binfluencer

Never miss bin day again.

Binfluencer tells you which bin goes out next, and gives you a nudge the night before. Type your postcode, pick your address, and that's it. No account, no sign-up.

What it does

  • Next bin at a glance: which bin goes out next, and when.
  • Night-before reminders: "Blue bin out tonight" at a time you choose.
  • Which bin? Search for an item, like a pizza box or a glass jar, and see where it goes, using your council's own rules.
  • More than one address: home, family, a holiday let.
  • Calendar: add your collections to your phone's calendar and it keeps itself up to date.

Where it works

Binfluencer currently covers Argyll and Bute. More Scottish councils are on the way, and the ones people ask for most get added first.

Binfluencer is an independent app and is not run by or affiliated with any council. Collection dates come from the information councils publish. If something looks wrong, your council's website is always the final word.

For councils

We'd love to work with you. Councils can send us their collection data directly through the Binfluencer API, so residents always see official dates, including holiday changes. Get in touch using the email below.

Privacy
